# Northwind Robotics — Employee Handbook (sample)

This is sample data for trying ragleitus. Upload it, then ask questions such as
"How many days of annual leave do I get?" or "What is the on-call allowance?".

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
plus public holidays. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year
and must be used by March 31st. Sick leave is not capped, but absences longer
than three consecutive days need a doctor's note.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on at least
one shared office day. Home-office equipment is reimbursed up to 600 EUR once
every three years.

## On-call

Engineers on the on-call rotation receive an allowance of 250 EUR per week on
call, plus time off in lieu for any incident handled between 22:00 and 07:00.
Rotations are one week long and hand over on Mondays at 10:00.

## Expenses

Travel must be booked through the travel portal. Economy class is the default
for flights under six hours. Receipts must be submitted within 30 days.
